# Privacy Policy — YT Cleaner

**Last updated:** July 2026  
**Developer:** kldev01

## Overview

YT Cleaner is a browser extension that modifies the YouTube interface locally
in your browser. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any
personal data.

## Data Collection

YT Cleaner does **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, IP address, location, etc.)
- Browsing history or activity
- YouTube account information
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Any other data of any kind

## Local Storage

All extension settings (enabled features and their configurations) are stored
exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and never
leave your device. This data is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

YT Cleaner does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising
services.

The extension operates entirely on `youtube.com` and does not communicate with
any external servers.

## Donations

The extension includes an optional link to a donation page. Visiting that page
is entirely voluntary and governed by the terms of the respective donation
platform. YT Cleaner itself does not process any payments or financial data.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL.
Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the
updated policy.
